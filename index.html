About Us

A dependable maintenance team for your property

FixMate Services provides reliable and professional property maintenance and handyman services across South and Central London. We specialise in home repairs, maintenance, carpentry, plumbing, basic electrical work, tiling, painting, furniture assembly and general property improvements.

We focus on quality workmanship, attention to detail, punctuality and customer satisfaction. From small household repairs to larger property maintenance projects, we aim to provide a professional and dependable service from start to finish.

Experience

Experienced in residential property maintenance and repair work, including doors, locks, shelving, cabinets, plumbing maintenance, sink installation, furniture assembly, painting preparation, silicone finishing, electrical cable work and general home improvements.
